Turn Any Device into a Beautiful Vintage Clock

Vintage Clock is a free online clock customizer that lets you create an elegant clock directly in your browser. Upload a favorite antique dial, choose the hands, adjust the colors, select a chime, and enjoy the finished clock in full-screen mode.

It was designed for anyone who loves antique clocks, vintage home décor, traditional craftsmanship, or the idea of giving an unused screen a new purpose.

Built with Privacy in Mind

Vintage Clock does not require an account. Your uploaded images and custom clock settings remain on your device and are stored locally in your browser whenever possible.

Your clock belongs to you. The app is designed to work without sending your personal clock-face images to a cloud account.

Why Vintage Clock?

Many online clocks simply tell the time. Vintage Clock was made for something more personal: recreating the feeling of owning and enjoying a beautiful traditional clock.

Whether you are displaying a family heirloom, experimenting with an antique dial, decorating a room, or repurposing an old tablet, Vintage Clock gives you a simple and free way to create a clock that feels like your own.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Vintage Clock free?

Yes. Vintage Clock is free to use in your web browser.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use the clock without creating an account or subscription.

Can I upload my own clock face?

Yes. You can use your own clock-face image and combine it with customizable hands and other settings.

Does it work on an iPad or Android tablet?

Yes. Many people can use a tablet as a dedicated full-screen vintage clock or wall display.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

Vintage Clock is designed to keep your images and settings on your own device rather than placing them in a personal cloud account.

Can I install Vintage Clock?

On supported devices, Vintage Clock can be added to the Home Screen and used like an app.

The Story Behind Vintage Clock

Vintage Clock began with a simple idea: many beautiful antique clocks are no longer able to keep accurate time, yet their craftsmanship, history, and character still deserve to be enjoyed.

Instead of allowing a treasured dial or old clock case to disappear, a modern screen can give it a new life. Vintage Clock makes it possible to preserve, recreate, and enjoy the appearance of classic clocks using a phone, tablet, or computer.

It is not about replacing antique clocks. It is about celebrating them and making their beauty accessible in a new form.
